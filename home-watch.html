Why It Exists

Most home damage starts small and slow.

A loose flashing turns into a $15,000 roof replacement. A drip under the sink turns into rotted subfloor. A clogged dryer vent turns into a fire. The fix at month one costs $50. The fix at month eighteen costs five figures.

Home Watch is the licensed eyes-on-the-property checkup that catches issues while they're still cheap to fix.

What You Get

Every visit. Every quarter.

01

Written report

All 25 items rated Good, Monitor, or Action Needed. Photos of any issues. Recommended repair pricing on the spot. Saved digitally so you can track changes over time.

02

One free repair hour

A complimentary hour of labor every visit. Caulk a tub, replace a smoke detector, swap a filter, tighten a hose bib — the small fixes are included.

03

Quote on the spot

Bigger fixes get a real number while we're already there. Materials at cost. Labor priced fair. No second visit just to give you a quote.
