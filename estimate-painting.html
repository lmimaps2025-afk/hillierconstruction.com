Common Questions About Painting Costs

How much does interior house painting cost?+
Interior painting typically costs $1,500 to $5,000 for a full home. Individual rooms run $300 to $900. Price depends on ceiling height, number of coats needed, and surface prep required.
How much does exterior painting cost?+
Exterior painting typically costs $1,800 to $6,500 for most homes. Price is influenced by home size, number of stories, siding type, and amount of prep work (scraping, priming) needed.
How long does professional painting take?+
A single room takes about one day. A full house interior takes 3 to 7 days. Exterior projects typically take 2 to 5 days depending on size and weather conditions.
What paint do professional painters use?+
Professional painters use premium paints from brands like Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, or similar quality brands. Premium paint costs more but covers better, lasts longer, and looks superior to big-box store paint.
