The Hillier Story

Oregon roots.
Aspen standards.
Back home.

Most contractors in Salem learned their trade locally. Byron Hillier learned his in Aspen, Colorado — where clients are demanding, budgets are real, and sloppy work gets you off the job site permanently.

High-end resort construction teaches you something no trade school does: what “finished” actually means. How joints should sit. How paint should lay. How a demo crew should leave a property.

Born and raised in Oregon, Byron came back to Salem to build for the people he grew up with — bringing every standard he earned in one of the most demanding construction markets in the country.
