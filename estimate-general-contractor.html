Common Questions About Remodeling Costs

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in 2026?+
Kitchen remodels range from $15,000 to $75,000+ depending on scope and finish level. A minor refresh with new cabinet faces and countertops runs $15,000 to $28,000. A full gut remodel with new layout and custom cabinets runs $40,000 to $75,000+.
How much does a bathroom remodel cost?+
Bathroom remodels typically cost $8,000 to $35,000. A basic refresh runs $8,000 to $15,000. A full master bath remodel with tile shower, double vanity, and soaking tub runs $20,000 to $40,000.
Do I need permits for a remodel?+
Most remodeling work involving structural changes, plumbing, or electrical requires permits. A licensed general contractor pulls all required permits as part of the project. Never hire a contractor who suggests skipping permits.
How long does a remodel take?+
Bathroom remodels take 2 to 4 weeks. Kitchen remodels take 4 to 8 weeks. Room additions take 2 to 4 months. Timeline depends on permit approval, material lead times, and project complexity.
