Common Questions About Excavation Costs

How much does excavation cost per hour?+
Excavation contractors typically charge $80 to $150 per hour for equipment and operator. Most jobs are quoted as a flat rate for the project rather than hourly. A site visit is required to give accurate flat-rate pricing because soil conditions and access vary significantly.
How much does yard grading cost?+
Yard grading typically costs $500 to $5,000 for residential projects. Simple drainage grading for a small yard runs $500 to $1,500. Grading a full lot for new construction or major slope correction runs $2,000 to $8,000+. Rocky or hard-to-access sites cost more.
What affects excavation cost the most?+
The biggest cost factors are project size, soil type (rock costs 2 to 3 times more than soft soil), site access for equipment, depth required, and debris disposal. Rocky soil or limited equipment access can significantly increase costs compared to soft, open sites.
Do I need a permit for excavation work?+
Many excavation projects require permits, especially for foundation work, grading near property lines, drainage alterations, and projects near waterways. A licensed contractor identifies all permit requirements and handles the application. Never start excavation work without checking permit requirements first.
How do I know if my property has underground utilities?+
Always call 811 (Oregon's dig safe line) before any excavation. This free service marks underground utility lines within 2 business days. Hitting a buried utility line is extremely dangerous and expensive. A licensed excavation contractor always calls 811 before breaking ground.
