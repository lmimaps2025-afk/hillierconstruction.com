Foundation Repair

A 1940s home, brought back from the edge.

The original footing was failing on one side of the house. Most contractors won't take this on — it requires shoring the structure, excavating tight to the home, pouring new footings, and tying it all back into the existing build without cracking finishes upstairs.

We did the full scope in-house. Excavation, shoring, structural, concrete, restoration. No subcontractor finger-pointing.

Deck Rebuild

A failing deck, rebuilt to last.

The old deck had reached end of life — soft boards, failing rails, and a step setup that wasn't safe. We tore it down, rebuilt the substructure, ran new decking, and added powder-coated metal rails for a finish that won't need re-staining every other summer.

The kind of straightforward job that still benefits from a licensed crew. Code-compliant rails, proper footings, real flashing where the deck meets the house.

Scope Tear-out and rebuild
Materials Cedar + metal rail
